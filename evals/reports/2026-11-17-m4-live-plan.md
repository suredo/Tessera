# Measures, frozen before the run

- **AI-dependent steps:** the **29 requests** (say steps) across the twelve runs. Release acceptance asks that **at least 85%, so 25 of 29,** pass their host facts without the person retrying or rephrasing; automatic repairs are allowed, and counted and reported apart (A40). Reported per scenario too, not only pooled.
- **Deterministic lifecycle steps:** the other **54 steps**, **37 do steps** and **17 others** (reload, next day, undo, save, switch, Use again, Continue). **Every one must pass.**
- **Denominators are fixed:** a step that can't run because an earlier one failed stays counted, as failed.
- **Profile matching** (#351's last criterion, `VER-03`): for each of the **14 requests that make a tool** (T10 and T10 in Portuguese make two each), whether the intent named the profile in the table, or none for T13. M3's rule stands: **fewer than 1 in 10 wrong, so one wrong in 14 passes and two fail it.** Read from the recorded answers.
- **Behavior checks:** for each version of a tool that fits a profile, whether its check passed at once, after its repair, or not at all; for each failure, whether its diagnostic says what was expected, what was observed and whose rule it is (`VER-07`).
- **No false success:** every step's host message is compared with what happened; one that claims a change that didn't happen, or a tool that doesn't work, fails release acceptance whatever the counts.
- **No unapproved data loss** (`DATA-10`): from the data hashes of each step and the undo records.
- **Cost and speed** (`OBS-03`, `OBS-04`): calls, tokens (cached apart) and model time per stage, and per request the checks reached and passed, from `evals/src/runner/metrics.ts` and `speed.ts` over the ledgers.
- **What it made:** the AI technical review of each run (`EVAL-03`), then the owner's verdict on each: accept, accept with notes, or reject (`EVAL-04`).

**The reserve is for repairs, and is partial on purpose,** as in M3:
- **What the pipeline allows:** each version made or changed may have one repair of each kind of failure: data shape, static, smoke, first sight and behavior (`REPAIR_BUDGETS`, `GEN-09`).

- **What happens past it:** a scenario's hard cap may stop it before every allowed repair is spent. That failure is counted, never retried within this cap.

- **Each run has its own hard cap,** set on its command (`--live --cap <n>`) and enforced by the harness, never above its scenario budget less its review. **Each review's cap is 1** (`node evals/src/review/cli.ts <run folder> --live --cap 1`).

# Stop rules

As the protocol says:
- **Step 1 of a scenario fails:** that scenario stops; the next one still runs.
- **The same step fails twice:** stop that step, fix the cause offline, and ask before spending more.
- **A provider error** (sign-in, unavailable model): stop the run.
- **A usage limit:** pause, then resume the remaining scenarios after it resets.
- **The same failure in two scenarios:** stop, to fix the cause offline before spending more.
