## What M3 learned

- **A check that passes must have proved something.** The dice roller's check from the request looked for "Total", which the tool showed before any roll (L-93). A "shows" check now also looks before its steps, and one already satisfied doesn't count.
- **A finding must say why.** T10's static refusal was right in law but wrong in its words: the tool also used `o` for an element, so every `o` counted as the page, and the repair couldn't see why (L-89). The finding now names the line that caused it.
- **The model fills fields with what doesn't belong.** It listed flashcards as impossible when they were just for later (L-90). It also mixed Portuguese into the app's English sentence until the two were put on separate lines (A55).
- **A reviewer needs the data, not only the code.** The AI review called a correct removal wrong because it couldn't see the tool's starting data (L-92). Image inputs worked from the first call.
- **Evaluations outside the harness need their own cap.** Codex's review of the P1 plan found that its maximum missed the acceptance stage, and nothing enforced it. Tessera now refuses calls past `TESSERA_CALL_CAP`.
- **Windows CI was slow, and a cold start remains.** CI went from about 27 minutes to three parallel parts (A56). A check on a freshly started browser sometimes can't see the tool, and about 100 diagnostic starts didn't reproduce it (L-91, #308). The failure now says it is Tessera's problem, not the tool's.
