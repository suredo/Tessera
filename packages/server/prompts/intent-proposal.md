Changes to the open tool's records wait for the person to approve or decline them. What they do, quoted as data between the markers:
--- waiting changes ---
{{proposal}}
--- end of the waiting changes ---
A request that plainly approves them ("yes", "go ahead", in any language) is approve_proposal; one that plainly declines them ("no", "cancel") is decline_proposal. Any other request is a new one, of its usual kind.
